Insurance Claims & Roof Inspections

Expert Roof Inspections & Insurance Claim Support

When unexpected roof damage strikes, navigating your insurance claim shouldn't be another headache. At Gomes's Construction, we're more than contractors. We're your partners in protecting your home and your investment.

Our team specializes in detailed roof inspections, thorough documentation, and skilled negotiation with your insurance provider to help you secure the coverage you deserve.

Why Homeowners Trust Gomes's Construction

  1. 01

    Accurate Inspections

    We conduct thorough, professional roof inspections, identifying all visible and hidden damage caused by wind, hail, or storms.

  2. 02

    Full Documentation

    You'll receive clear photo evidence, written reports, and code-based assessments: everything your adjuster needs to process your claim properly.

  3. 03

    Direct Negotiation

    We communicate directly with your insurance company on your behalf, making sure nothing is overlooked and your payout reflects the true scope of the damage.

  4. 04

    Trusted Repairs & Replacements

    Once your claim is approved, we get to work, delivering quality roofing solutions built to last, using premium materials and expert installation.
