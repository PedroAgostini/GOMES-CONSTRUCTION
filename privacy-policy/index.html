Who we are

Gomes's Construction Inc. ("Gomes's Construction", "we", "us") is a roofing and siding contractor based at 58 Denton St, Brockton, MA 02301, registered as a Massachusetts Home Improvement Contractor (registration 201976). This policy covers gomessroofing.com and the landing pages we use for our advertising.

Information we collect

Information you give us

When you request an estimate, call, text or email us, you may share:

  • Contact details: your name, phone number and email address.
  • Property details: the address or ZIP code of the property and the service you need.
  • Project details: anything you choose to tell us about the job, plus photos or insurance claim information you send us later.

Information collected automatically

Like most websites, our hosting provider records basic technical data when pages load, such as your IP address, browser type, the pages you visit and the time of the visit. Some of the third party tools described below also collect device and usage information.

We do not ask for, and you should not send through our forms, Social Security numbers, bank account numbers or credit card numbers.

How we use it

  • To answer your request, schedule an inspection and prepare your estimate.
  • To plan, carry out and follow up on your project, including warranty service.
  • To help with your insurance claim when you ask us to.
  • To keep records required for our business, taxes and licensing.
  • To understand which ads and pages bring people to us, so we can improve them.
  • To keep the website secure and filter out spam.

Calls, texts and emails

When you send an estimate request, you agree that we may contact you by phone call, text message or email about that request and your project. Message and data rates may apply. You can reply STOP to any text, or simply tell us, and we will stop texting you. Agreeing to be contacted is never a condition of buying anything from us.

We do not add you to marketing lists or send promotional messages unless you ask us to.

When we share it

We do not sell your personal information. We only share it with:

  • Service providers that help us run the website and the business, such as our web host and Web3Forms, which delivers form submissions to our email. They may only use it to provide their service to us.
  • Our crew and suppliers who need the property address and project details to deliver materials and do the work.
  • Manufacturers, such as CertainTeed, when we register a product warranty for your home.
  • Your insurance company, only when you ask us to help with a claim.
  • Authorities, when the law requires it, or to protect our rights, our customers or the public.

Cookies and similar tools

Our own pages do not set cookies. To prevent spam, the estimate form keeps a short record of when it was sent in your browser's local storage. That record never leaves your device.

Some features come from other companies, which may set their own cookies or collect usage data under their own privacy policies:

  • Google Maps, for the map of our location.
  • Google Fonts, for the typefaces on the site.
  • Advertising and measurement tools from Google (such as Google Tag Manager and Google Ads) and Meta, used on some pages to see which ads lead to estimate requests.

You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings, and manage ad personalization in your Google ad settings and Meta ad preferences.

How long we keep it

We keep estimate requests and project records for as long as we need them to serve you, honor our labor guarantee and warranties, and meet legal, tax and licensing obligations. Requests that do not turn into a project are deleted when we no longer need them.

How we protect it

The website runs over an encrypted HTTPS connection, and only the people who need your information to handle your request or project can access it. No method of sending or storing data is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security, but we take reasonable steps to protect what you share with us.

Your choices

You can ask us at any time to:

  • Tell you what personal information we have about you.
  • Correct information that is wrong.
  • Delete your information, unless we must keep it for legal, tax, contract or warranty reasons.
  • Stop contacting you by phone, text or email.

Send your request using the contact details below. We may need to confirm your identity before we act on it.

Children

Our services are for homeowners and property owners. The website is not meant for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their information. If you believe a child has sent us information, contact us and we will delete it.

Other websites

Our site links to other websites, such as Google reviews and Google Maps. We are not responsible for how those websites handle your information, so please read their privacy policies.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. When we do, we will change the effective date at the top of this page. Using the website after an update means you accept the updated policy.
