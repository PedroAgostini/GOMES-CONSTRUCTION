What Sets Us Apart

Every project begins with a detailed inspection and clear communication. We install complete single brand roofing systems designed to withstand heavy snow and severe winds. With our 40-year material warranty and 10-year labor guarantee, you get total peace of mind and lasting structural integrity.

  1. 01Solid Deck

    Plywood CDX. We check the decking and replace soft or rotted wood before anything goes on top.

  2. 02Ice & Water Shield

    CertainTeed WinterGuard®. A self-sealing membrane that guards the roof edges, where New England ice dams start.

  3. 03Synthetic Underlayment

    CertainTeed RoofRunner™. A second line of defense across the whole deck, from the same manufacturer as your shingles.

  4. 04Starter Strip

    CertainTeed SwiftStart®. Starter shingles at the eaves seal the first course against wind uplift.

  5. 05Architectural Shingles

    CertainTeed Landmark® PRO. Dimensional shingles, installed to manufacturer specifications so the roof is covered as one system.

  6. 06Ridge Cap

    CertainTeed Shadow Ridge®. Matching ridge cap shingles finish and seal the peak of the roof.
